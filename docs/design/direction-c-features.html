Audio output switcher spec 006

A new Sound tab in the command sheet (and the chip in cover view) move sound between TV speakers, soundbar, Bluetooth or headphones in one tap. The active output is marked; unavailable ones grey out only after the TV rejects them.

Seamless reconnect spec 008

Lock/unlock should be a visual non-event. The remote stays on screen and usable through a short grace window; the full "can't reach your TV" screen appears only when the TV is genuinely gone.

0ms

Unlock → already there

The last-known remote is still rendered. Tap immediately and the action is sent or held for the sub-second reconnect. No spinner, no rebuild.
