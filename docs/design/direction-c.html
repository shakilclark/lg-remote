Glide & tap

Move your thumb to drive the TV pointer; tap anywhere to click. The same motion-cursor engine that's already built.

⇅

Edges = volume & channel

Swipe the right edge for volume, the left for channel. Hold to ramp. No buttons to aim at — the most-used controls are muscle memory.

←

Swipe-in to go back

A short swipe from the screen edge is Back; the mic and power stay pinned top-right where you won't hit them by accident.

⇧

Pull up for everything else

The grip is a real handle — drag it (or tap it) to raise the command sheet. Discoverable on sight, no gesture to learn.
